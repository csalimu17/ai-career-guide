# AI Career Guide Brand Guide

## Brand

AI Career Guide is a practical AI workspace for building stronger CVs, improving ATS match rates, generating cover letters, preparing for interviews, finding jobs, and managing a job search.

Primary domain: `https://aicareerguide.uk`

Support email: `info@aicareerguide.uk`

## Audience

- UK job seekers who need clearer CVs, better application targeting, and practical career support.
- Users may be anxious, time-constrained, or unsure how to translate their experience into strong applications.
- The product should feel capable, trustworthy, and easy to act on.

## Voice

- Clear, expert, calm, and direct.
- Outcome-focused: explain what the user can improve and why it matters.
- Practical over hype. Avoid exaggerated claims, vague AI buzzwords, and pressure-heavy copy.
- Use British English where natural for public-facing CV and job-search content.

## Visual System

- Core brand name: `AI Career Guide`.
- Primary gradient: purple to teal to coral, defined in `src/app/globals.css`.
- Tailwind brand colours live in `tailwind.config.ts`:
  - Purple: `#7d2ae8`
  - Teal: `#00c4cc`
  - Magenta: `#e040fb`
  - Blue: `#1976D2`
  - Orange: `#FF9800`
- Fonts are configured in `src/app/layout.tsx`: Plus Jakarta Sans, Manrope, and Sora.
- Layout should feel polished, spacious, and readable, with strong responsive behaviour on mobile.
- Use gradients deliberately for important moments, not as filler.
- Preserve accessible contrast, visible focus states, and readable text sizes.

## Product Experience

- Lead with useful workflows, not marketing decoration.
- Make primary actions obvious and honest.
- Use clear loading, empty, success, and error states.
- Avoid layout shifts, clipped text, hidden controls, or controls that only work on desktop.
- Keep CV, ATS, editor, export, and print experiences especially stable because they carry high user trust.

## SEO And Content

- Key topic areas: AI CV builder, free CV builder, ATS CV checker, resume builder, CV templates, AI career assistant, cover letter generator, interview prep, job search, and UK job search.
- Content should answer real search intent and help users complete a career task.
- Avoid keyword stuffing, duplicate page intent, generic filler, or unsupported ranking claims.
- Metadata, headings, sitemap entries, internal links, and Open Graph assets should stay consistent with the product promise.
