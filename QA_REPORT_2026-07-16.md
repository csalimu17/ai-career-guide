## Confirmed defects and repairs

### ACG-001 — High — Internal editorial commentary rendered publicly

- Routes: `/cv-builder`, `/blog/[slug]`; persona: visitor.
- Reproduction: open the pages and read the body/aside copy.
- Expected: customer-facing guidance only.
- Actual: editorial phrases such as “Article brief”, “That is the job this page is designed to do”, and search-ranking commentary were visible.
- Root cause: internal drafting copy was committed directly in page JSX.
- Fix: replaced it with natural workflow and learning-outcome copy; repository search confirms the supplied phrases no longer occur in rendered source.
- Regression: `conversion-regression.spec.ts` checks public pages for the known commentary patterns.
- Status: fixed; mobile and desktop regression passed.

### ACG-002 — High — Signup lost or weakly validated conversion intent

- Routes: marketing CTA → `/signup` or `/login`; persona: new and returning user.
- Reproduction: select a CV, upload, ATS, template, or plan action and authenticate.
- Expected: resume the selected workflow without an unsafe redirect or repeated selection.
- Actual: email signup forced `/onboarding`; values varied (`scratch`, `upload`); arbitrary same-origin paths passed the old prefix-only redirect check; paid intent returned to pricing.
- Root cause: duplicated session-only logic in login/signup plus an unconditional email-signup redirect.
- Fix: added one typed intent parser, template/plan/value validation, an exact internal path allowlist, durable local persistence for refresh/OAuth return, direct workflow destinations, and one-time paid checkout resumption.
- Regression: validates create/upload/ATS/template/plan destinations and rejects an external return URL.
- Status: fixed in code; authenticated Firebase/Stripe completion remains deferred pending safe test accounts and Stripe test credentials.

### ACG-003 — High — AI credential prefixes appeared in build logs

- Route: server build/startup; persona: operator.
- Expected: credentials never appear in logs.
- Actual: first four characters of configured provider credentials were printed repeatedly during static generation.
- Root cause: diagnostic logging in `src/ai/genkit.ts`.
- Fix: removed credential-derived and environment-presence logging. Final build emitted neither prefixes nor provider-key diagnostics.
- Status: fixed and build-verified.

### ACG-004 — Medium — CTA labels and destinations were inconsistent

- Routes: public header, CV builder, ATS checker, templates, pricing.
- Fix: generic public CTAs now use “Build My CV Free” with `intent=create-cv`; contextual actions retain “Check My CV”, “Use This Template”, plan-specific labels, and agency pricing routes to support instead of signup.
- Regression: rendered-link assertions on CV builder, ATS checker, pricing, and templates.
- Status: fixed.

### ACG-005 — Medium — Type-check baseline failed

- Actual: nullable Upstash Redis test client was dereferenced in chaos tests.
- Fix: retained the existing environment guard and added explicit non-null narrowing at the guarded calls.
- Status: fixed; type-check passes.
